How rankings work

Your list, and how everyone’s lists become the Global one.

Categories

Clones and near-clones, like Vekoma Boomerangs, SLCs and Wacky Worms, share one row, so you rank the design once instead of 20 times. Every one you’ve ridden, now or later, takes that spot. See all categories.

The Global list

Everyone’s lists combined into one score out of 100.

  • Place among your credits. Your #1 scores 100, and lower spots score less, measured against everything you’ve ridden. Unranked rides count as below your ranked ones, so ranking just your top 100 is enough.
  • More credits, more say. A list’s weight is the square root of the rider’s credits: 100 credits weighs 10, and 400 or more weighs the most, 20.
  • Outliers dropped. From 5 lists a ride’s lowest score is left out, and from 20 lists the lowest tenth.
  • A pull toward the middle. A small extra vote at the site average keeps a ride on three perfect lists from jumping straight to #1.

Your list

Best to worst, in your order; nothing reorders it. Rank more places a ride by asking which was better?, about ten questions even on a 500-ride list. It starts with your tallest unranked credits and mixes in categories and rides other riders have ranked.

“You agree on”

On another rider’s list: how often you both put the same two rides in the same order. The biggest split is the ride your lists disagree on most.
