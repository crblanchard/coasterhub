## Description [4000]

Coaster Hub is the home for your roller coaster count.

LOG A PARK DAY IN SECONDS
Open the app at the park and it already knows where you are. Tap + once per ride, re-rides included, and your day is saved, even with no signal. It sends itself when you're back online.

RANK YOUR FAVORITES THE EASY WAY
No dragging 500 rides into order. Rank more asks one question at a time: which was better? Each answer halves the list, so even a huge count takes about ten taps per ride. Near-identical rides like Boomerangs and SLCs share one spot, so you rank the design once.

SEE WHAT EVERYONE THINKS
Every rider's list combines into the Global rankings, weighted so experience counts but nobody outvotes everyone else.

FOLLOW YOUR FRIENDS
See who rode what, where you agree, and where your lists split. Compare counts side by side.

EVERY COASTER, EVERY PARK
Height, speed, length, inversions, opening dates, manufacturer and model for each ride, with maps of where you've been and which coasters are nearest.

YOUR COUNT, YOUR WAY
- Credits, rides and parks, counted automatically
- Import an existing list or spreadsheet
- A map of everywhere you've ridden
- Light and dark mode that follows your phone

Free, no ads, no tracking.

## App Review information

- **Sign-in required**: yes. Make a reviewer account before submitting:
  sign up at coasterhub.org with an email you control, log a park day and
  rank a few rides so the screens aren't empty, and put the email and
  password here. Never put the password in a chat or in this repo.
- **Notes for the reviewer** (paste):

  Coaster Hub lets roller coaster fans log the rides they've been on, rank them, and follow friends. To try it: sign in with the account above, tap Log to record a ride, and open Rankings to rank. Account deletion: Me → Account → Delete account. To report or block a rider: open any other rider's profile and tap ⋯ at the top right. Profile names and bios are filtered for objectionable words, and reports are emailed to the developer, who acts on them within 24 hours. Location is used only on the device, to pick the park you're at.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Data used to track you:** none.

**Data linked to you** (all for *App Functionality*, none for tracking,
advertising or analytics):
- Contact Info → Email Address
- User Content → Photos or Videos (profile picture)
- User Content → Other User Content (rides, rankings, bio)
- Identifiers → User ID (your username)

**Not collected:** Location (the app uses it on the phone only and never
sends it), Usage Data, Diagnostics, Purchases, Browsing History, Health,
Financial info, Contacts, Sensitive info.
