Privacy Policy

What Coaster Hub keeps, and how to delete your account.

Last updated October 30, 2026.

The short version

Coaster Hub keeps what it needs to run your account and show your coaster count, and nothing else. There are no ads, no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What’s collected

  • Your account: your email address and your password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form).
  • Your profile: display name, username, bio and picture, if you add them. These are public.
  • Your riding: the coasters you’ve ridden, the dates, your rankings and categories, and who you follow. These are public.
  • Activity: a public log of changes (a day logged, a ranking saved), shown on /changes and in feeds.
  • Reports and blocks you make, which only Coaster Hub’s admins can see.

What isn’t

  • Your location never leaves your device. If you allow it, the site uses it in your browser to find the park you’re at. It isn’t sent to or stored on Coaster Hub’s servers.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers, and no third-party cookies.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps you signed in. Your device also stores a few small preferences (like which rider you last looked at) so pages open the way you left them.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores its database and pictures.
  • Resend sends the password-reset emails you ask for.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri draw the maps. Your browser asks them for map pictures, which shows them your IP address the way any website visit does.

None of them get your data for their own use.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, on the website or in the app:

  1. Sign in at coasterhub.org/account. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password there first.
  2. Open your profile and tap Account at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

That deletes your account, email, password, profile, picture, rides, rankings, categories, follows, blocks and activity right away. Nothing is kept. If you can’t sign in at all, email hello@coasterhub.org from the address on the account and it will be deleted for you.

Children

Coaster Hub isn’t meant for children under 13, and doesn’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email the address below and it will be removed.
