Sources

Where the coaster data on Coaster Hub comes from.

Every coaster stat on Coaster Hub (height, speed, length, inversions, ride time, manufacturer, model, opening and closing dates) comes from open data that anyone can reuse, or was entered by hand. Each value records which source it came from. The open sources are checked again every week, so new and corrected information arrives on its own.
