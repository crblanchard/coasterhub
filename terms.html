The short version

Coaster Hub is a free place to log the roller coasters you’ve ridden, rank them, and follow other riders. By creating an account or using the site or the app, you agree to these terms. Be decent to each other, keep it about coasters, and don’t try to break anything.

Your account

  • You need to be at least 13 to make an account.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You’re responsible for what happens on your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile: Account → Delete account. That removes it and everything in it for good.

What you post

Your name, username, picture, bio, rides and rankings are public: anyone can see them. You keep ownership of what you post, and you let Coaster Hub show it on the site and in the app so the site works (your profile, the Global rankings, feeds).

No tolerance for objectionable content or abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users on Coaster Hub. Don’t post anything hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, threatening, or illegal, don’t pretend to be someone else, and don’t spam. Names and bios are filtered for offensive words, and anyone can report a profile or block a rider from the … button on their page.

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the account that posted it can be suspended or deleted without warning.

Coaster data

Coaster and park facts are gathered with care, but they can be wrong or out of date. Don’t rely on them for anything important. Corrections are welcome.
