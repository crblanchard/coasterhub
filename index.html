The idea

Why Coaster Hub

This started as one meticulously kept spreadsheet and grew into something a few of us share.

Every number on the site comes from a ride someone actually logged — no estimates, no rounding, nothing filled in from memory. That’s the point of keeping it this carefully: the totals are worth trusting, so the maps and records built on them are too. Every edit is on the record, too — see what’s changed lately.

Next up: a combined ranking built from everyone’s favorites, and accounts, so keeping a count here beats keeping it in a spreadsheet of your own.
