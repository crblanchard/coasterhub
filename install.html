Add Coaster Hub to your home screen.

You’re all set. You’re already using Coaster Hub from your home screen.
  1. Open coasterhub.org in Safari. Chrome on iPhone works too — its Share button is at the bottom right.
  2. Tap the Share button At the bottom of the screen — on newer iPhones, tap ··· first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add.
  1. Open coasterhub.org in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install. Coaster Hub is now on your home screen.
